# Isometric office, candidate concept frame

What the watcher sees at 8 bots. Isometric cutaway of one open-plan floor.

## Metaphor mapping

| concept | in the scene |
| --- | --- |
| bot | one desk pod |
| working | monitor glowing, desk lamp on, a figure typing |
| idle | monitor on but dim, figure leaning back in the chair |
| asleep | monitor black, lamp off, chair pushed in, desk empty |
| action label | a nameplate on the desk divider |
| roster overlay | translucent panel at the left edge, over the floorplan |

## Legibility at a glance

The glowing monitor is the working signal and reads at thumbnail size against the
matte floor. Desks are on a regular isometric grid with aisles between them so eight
pods are countable.

## All-asleep state

Every monitor black, every chair pushed in. The floor still reads as eight furnished
desks rather than an empty room.
