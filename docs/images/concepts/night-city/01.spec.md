# Night city, candidate concept frame

What the watcher sees at 8 bots. Street-level elevation of a night skyline.

## Metaphor mapping

| concept | in the scene |
| --- | --- |
| bot | one tower in the skyline |
| working | many lit windows and a burning rooftop beacon |
| idle | a few lit windows, beacon out |
| asleep | tower dark against the sky, one red aircraft light at the top |
| action label | a lit sign band across the tower's lower floors |
| roster overlay | translucent panel at the left edge, above the skyline |

## Legibility at a glance

Window density is the working signal. Towers differ in height and width so eight
are countable as separate silhouettes against the sky.

## All-asleep state

Every tower dark with one red light at its crown. The skyline still reads as eight
standing towers, and the row of red lights is the sleeping roster.
