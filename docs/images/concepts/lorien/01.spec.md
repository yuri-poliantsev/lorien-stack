# Lórien, candidate concept frame

What the watcher sees at 8 bots. Side view, layered parallax forest at dusk (Q16).

## Metaphor mapping

| concept | in the scene |
| --- | --- |
| bot | one wooden flet on a mallorn branch, at its own height |
| working | flet lantern burning warm gold, an elven figure standing at a writing stand |
| idle | lantern low and amber, figure seated and still |
| asleep | flet dark, lantern out, a single pale firefly pulse at the rail |
| action label | a small hanging wooden sign under each flet |
| roster overlay | translucent panel at the left edge, not part of the forest |

## Legibility at a glance

Working, idle and asleep must separate on lantern brightness alone, readable in a
two-second look at a phone-sized frame, without reading any label. Flets sit at
distinct heights so no two overlap and each silhouette is countable.

## All-asleep state

With every lantern out the frame still reads as eight inhabited platforms:
silhouettes stay against the lighter dusk sky, and the firefly pulses mark which
platforms exist. The scene must never look empty or broken when nothing is working.
