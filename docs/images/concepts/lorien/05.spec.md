# Lórien 05, morning end of the clock cycle

Variation under test: the other end of the Q16 real-clock cycle. If the theme only
works at dusk it is a wallpaper, not a clock-driven scene.

## Metaphor mapping

Same as `01.spec.md`, but the sky is early morning. Lanterns still carry status, and at
low sun they must stay readable against a bright sky rather than washing out.

## Legibility at a glance

Working lanterns must still separate from dark flets in daylight. This frame exists to
prove the status signal does not depend on a dark background.

## All-asleep state

A dark flet in morning light reads by its unlit lantern and shuttered look, not by
contrast with a night sky.
