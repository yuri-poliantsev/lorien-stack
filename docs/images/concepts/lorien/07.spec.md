# Lórien 07, close camera on legible nametags

Variation under test: Q18 says the in-scene label is pose plus action plus file path.
At the zoom-to-fit default those labels have to be legible. This frame is the close end
of the Q20 camera, showing what a hanging sign can actually hold.

## Metaphor mapping

Same as `01.spec.md`. Each hanging wooden sign carries two lines: a short name and a
shorter status word beneath it.

## Legibility at a glance

Signs are large enough to read, and the working versus asleep distinction still comes
from the lantern rather than from the sign.

## All-asleep state

Signs remain lit enough to read when the lantern is out, because the roster must stay
readable while nothing is working.
