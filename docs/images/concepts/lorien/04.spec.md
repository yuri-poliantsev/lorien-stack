# Lórien 04, all-asleep at deep night

Variation under test: the constraint the whole product rests on. Every bot asleep, and
the frame must still read as eight inhabited platforms rather than an empty forest.
This is also the far end of the Q16 real-clock cycle.

## Metaphor mapping

Every flet in the asleep state: lantern out, boards dark, one pale firefly pulse at the
rail, hanging wooden sign still visible. No figures standing.

## Legibility at a glance

The watcher must be able to count eight platforms with every lantern out. Firefly
pulses are the only warm light in the frame and they mark the roster.

## All-asleep state

This frame is the all-asleep state.
