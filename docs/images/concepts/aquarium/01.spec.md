# Aquarium, candidate concept frame

What the watcher sees at 8 bots. Straight-on elevation through the glass of a
planted reef tank.

## Metaphor mapping

| concept | in the scene |
| --- | --- |
| bot | one anemone with its resident fish, on its own rock |
| working | anemone open and brightly coloured, fish swimming above it |
| idle | anemone open but pale, fish hovering close to the rock |
| asleep | anemone closed to a knot, fish tucked into the rock, colour drained |
| action label | a small engraved brass plate on the glass under each rock |
| roster overlay | translucent panel at the left edge, over the glass |

## Legibility at a glance

Open versus closed anemone is a silhouette change, readable without colour. Rocks
are spaced along the sand so eight stations are countable.

## All-asleep state

Every anemone closed, every fish tucked in, the water dim. The tank still reads as
eight occupied rocks rather than bare stone.
