# StarCraft rework 02, all-asleep outpost

The constraint the product rests on: 18 real bots, mostly asleep. Every building dark,
one roof beacon each, and the frame still reads as eight occupied plots.

## Metaphor mapping

Every building in the asleep state per `01.spec.md`: dark interior, one small beacon on
the roof, front plate still faintly lit. No worker units anywhere.

## Legibility at a glance

The eight beacons are the sleeping roster. They must be countable as eight distinct
points of light without reading anything.
