# StarCraft rework 03, workers legible at the working buildings

Variation under test: Q15 says a worker appears and works when active. This frame pulls
the camera close enough to prove the worker is legible as a status signal rather than
decoration lost in the terrain.

## Metaphor mapping

Same as `01.spec.md`, with the worker unit large enough to read its pose.

## Legibility at a glance

Two rows of four buildings, workers only at the lit ones. A watcher must be able to say
which buildings have a worker without zooming.

## All-asleep state

Removing every worker and darkening every interior must leave the grid intact.
