# StarCraft rework 01, generative grid at 8 bots

Q15 in one frame. An original science-fiction industrial outpost, not a copy of any
published game's art. Isometric real-time-strategy camera.

## Metaphor mapping

| concept | in the scene |
| --- | --- |
| bot | one building on its own terrain plot |
| working | building lit from within, a worker unit standing at it doing something |
| idle | building lit but no worker present |
| asleep | building dark, one small beacon light blinking on its roof |
| action label | a lit plate on the building's front face |
| roster overlay | translucent panel at the left edge, over the terrain |

## Legibility at a glance

Buildings sit on a generative grid with clear terrain between plots, so eight are
countable without tracing. Interior light versus a single roof beacon is the whole
status signal and it survives at thumbnail size.

## All-asleep state

Eight dark buildings, each with one roof beacon. The outpost reads as built and occupied
rather than destroyed or empty.
