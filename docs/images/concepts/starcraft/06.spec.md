# StarCraft rework 06, wide camera with crowding headroom

Variation under test: Q4 puts the roster at 1 to 40 with no overlap up to 24 and
graceful crowding above. This frame pulls the camera back so the empty plots that a
larger roster would fill are visible, and the eight occupied plots still read.

## Metaphor mapping

Same as `01.spec.md`. Empty prepared plots are terrain, not bots, and must not be
mistakable for a building.

## Legibility at a glance

Eight buildings among clearly empty prepared plots. A watcher counts buildings, not
plots, so an empty plot must look plainly unbuilt.

## All-asleep state

Eight beacons among empty plots still separates occupied from unoccupied.
