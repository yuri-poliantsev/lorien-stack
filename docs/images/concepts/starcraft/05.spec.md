# StarCraft rework 05, nametag plates

Variation under test: Q18 says the in-scene label is pose plus action plus file path,
and Q4 puts 40 bots on screen. This frame proves a building can carry a readable plate
without the plate becoming the composition.

## Metaphor mapping

Same as `01.spec.md`, with a lit plate on each building's front face carrying a short
name on one line and a shorter status word beneath.

## Legibility at a glance

Plates are readable, and the lit-versus-beacon distinction still comes from the building
rather than from the plate text.

## All-asleep state

Plates stay faintly lit when interiors are dark, because the roster must stay readable
while nothing is working.
