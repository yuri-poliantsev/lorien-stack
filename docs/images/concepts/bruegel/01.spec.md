# Bruegel village, candidate concept frame

What the watcher sees at 8 bots. Elevated village panorama in the manner of a
Netherlandish winter painting.

## Metaphor mapping

| concept | in the scene |
| --- | --- |
| bot | one cottage workshop in the village |
| working | smoke rising from the chimney, a figure at work in the yard |
| idle | no smoke, a figure standing still at the door |
| asleep | shutters closed, chimney cold, one candle behind a window |
| action label | a painted wooden signboard hanging over each door |
| roster overlay | translucent panel at the left edge, not part of the painting |

## Legibility at a glance

Chimney smoke is the working signal and carries at thumbnail size. Cottages are
spread across the snow field with clear ground between them so eight are countable
without tracing outlines.

## All-asleep state

Every chimney cold, every shutter closed, one candle per cottage window. The village
still reads as eight inhabited houses under snow, not as a ruin.
