# Mission control 07, full shell with header and picker

Variation under test: Q9's shell redesign and Q14's picker. This frame shows the whole
surface the theme lives inside: header with a compact segmented theme picker, collapsed
roster rail, stats strip, and the card grid. It is the frame the shell owner reads.

## Metaphor mapping

Same as `01.spec.md`. The header and picker belong to the host, not the theme, but they
share the theme's dark surface, which is why they appear in this frame.

## Legibility at a glance

Header, strip, and grid form three horizontal bands with clear separation. Eight cards in
the grid, countable, with the chrome taking no more than the top fifth of the frame.

## All-asleep state

Chrome unchanged, all eight cards dimmed to outlines.
