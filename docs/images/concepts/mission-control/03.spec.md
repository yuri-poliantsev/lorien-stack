# Mission control 03, all eight asleep

The constraint the product rests on, in the dashboard idiom. Every card dimmed to an
outline with one dot, and the frame must still look deliberate rather than dead.

## Metaphor mapping

Every card in the asleep state per `01.spec.md`: thin grey outline, one small dot in the
corner, name still legible, no accent bar and no chart.

## Legibility at a glance

Eight outlines are countable and evenly placed. Nothing in the frame reads as an error,
an empty state, or a failure to load.

## All-asleep state

This frame is the all-asleep state.
