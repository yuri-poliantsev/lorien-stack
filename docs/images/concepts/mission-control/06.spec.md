# Mission control 06, dense list instead of a grid

Variation under test: Q4 puts the roster at 1 to 40. A card grid may not survive 40. A
full-width row list scales linearly, so this frame tests whether the theme still reads as
editorial rather than as a table.

## Metaphor mapping

Same as `01.spec.md`, except a bot is a full-width row instead of a card: accent bar at
the left edge when working, name in large type, a thin activity line at the right.

## Legibility at a glance

Eight rows stacked with generous leading. Working versus asleep is the accent bar, read
down the left edge as a single column of colour.

## All-asleep state

Eight rows with no accent bars still read as a deliberate list.

## Read-back checks

- require: dark | black | near-black | dark background
- require: eight | 8

- require: name | names | text | type | label | labels | heading | lettering
- require: bar | bars | accent | edge | stripe | stripes | line | lines
- require: dim | dimmed | muted | grey | gray | faint | dark
- forbid: crt | scanline | scanlines | phosphor | cathode
- forbid: grid of cards | pixel art | sprite
