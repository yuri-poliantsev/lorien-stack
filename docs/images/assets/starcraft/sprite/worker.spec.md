# StarCraft worker sprite

The unit that appears at a building when its bot is working (Q15). Generated on a flat
keyable background so `assets key` can produce the engine-ready PNG.

## Engine-ready defaults claimed

Isolated subject, flat single-colour keyable magenta ground, clean readable silhouette,
no baked cast shadow, no ground scene. Per `game-asset-core`, these are not stated by the
requester and are the lever's job.

## Read-back checks

- require: robot | worker | machine | mechanical | figure | droid
- require: magenta | pink | purple background | flat background
- require: single | one | alone | isolated | centred | centered
- forbid: shadow | ground | terrain | landscape
