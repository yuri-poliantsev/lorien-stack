# a-hut, the timber building shell

The lit state of one of two building variants, keyed to alpha and drawn on a concrete pad.
The asleep state is this same art with a dark wash composited over it in code, so the lit
windows and the doorway light have to be unmistakable here.

## Read-back checks

- require: isometric | axonometric | three-quarter | angled | elevated | perspective
- require: building | buildings | structure | shed | cabin | hut | barn | warehouse | depot | house
- require: wood | wooden | timber | plank | planks | orange | brown
- require: window | windows | door | doors | doorway
- require: lit | glowing | glow | light | lights | illuminated | yellow
- require: red
- require: magenta | pink | purple
- forbid: photograph
- forbid: photorealistic
