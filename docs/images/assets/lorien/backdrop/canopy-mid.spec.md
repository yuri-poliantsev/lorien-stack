# Lórien mid-canopy parallax layer

The middle parallax layer. Mallorn foliage hanging from the top edge only, so the
flets and the sky below it stay unobstructed. Keyed on magenta, so the flat
background has to be legible as magenta in the read-back.

## Read-back checks

- require: leaves | leaf | foliage | canopy
- require: top | upper | above | hanging | descending
- require: gold | yellow | green | silver
- require: magenta | pink | fuchsia
- require: empty | blank | bare | lower | bottom
- forbid: top-down
- forbid: overhead
- forbid: aerial
