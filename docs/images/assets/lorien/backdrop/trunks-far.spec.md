# Lórien far-trunk parallax layer

The deepest of three parallax layers. Colossal mallorn trunks seen straight on,
spaced far enough apart that the code-drawn dusk sky shows between them. Keyed on
magenta, so the flat background has to be legible as magenta in the read-back or the
layer cannot be made transparent.

## Read-back checks

- require: side | side-on | straight-on | profile | frontal | front-facing | eye level | elevation | vertical
- require: trunk | trunks | tree | trees | column | columns
- require: silver | grey | gray | pale | white
- require: magenta | pink | fuchsia
- require: gap | gaps | apart | spaced | between | separated
- forbid: top-down
- forbid: overhead
- forbid: aerial
