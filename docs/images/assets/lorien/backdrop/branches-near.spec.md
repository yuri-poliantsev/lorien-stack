# Lórien near-branch parallax layer

The nearest parallax layer, drawn in front of the flets. Dark branches crowding the
bottom corners only, so the centre of the frame where the flets live stays clear.
Keyed on magenta, so the flat background has to be legible as magenta in the
read-back.

## Read-back checks

- require: branch | branches | limb | limbs | bough | boughs
- require: dark | black | charcoal | silhouette | silhouetted | shadow
- require: corner | corners | left | right
- require: magenta | pink | fuchsia
- require: leaves | leaf | foliage | cluster | clusters
- forbid: top-down
- forbid: overhead
- forbid: aerial
