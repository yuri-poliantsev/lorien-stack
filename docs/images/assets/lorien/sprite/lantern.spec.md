# Lórien lantern

The lantern case only, unlit. Lit versus out is the primary working-versus-asleep
signal, and brightness is stateful, so the flame, the glow and the flicker are code
and the raster stays dark. Keyed on magenta, so the flat background has to be legible
as magenta in the read-back.

## Read-back checks

- require: lantern | lamp | luminaire | light fixture
- require: glass | pane | panes | case | casing | housing
- require: gold | brass | bronze | metal | metallic
- require: magenta | pink | fuchsia
- require: side | side-on | straight-on | profile | frontal | front-facing | eye level | elevation
- forbid: top-down
- forbid: overhead
- forbid: aerial
