# Lórien flet

One bot is one flet. The raster is the empty platform only: the figure, the lantern
glow, the nametag sign and every pose difference are code, per Q5. Seen straight on
from the side to match concept frame 02. Keyed on magenta, so the flat background has
to be legible as magenta in the read-back.

- require: brown | honey | gold | tan | amber | cream
- require: magenta | pink | fuchsia
- require: side | side-on | straight-on | profile | frontal | front-facing | eye level | elevation
- forbid: top-down
- forbid: overhead
